# Automancer Site — Vision

> **Authority 0.** This file outranks every other document in this repository — specs, roadmaps, ADRs, issues, and this repo's own agent instructions. Where one of them conflicts with this file, that document is wrong: correct it. Only Waseem changes this file, and only on his explicit instruction. An agent that thinks this file should change says so and waits.

## The destination

Automancer's public front door, trusted equally by people and by machines. Its job is to win work from people who have never met anyone here. A UK small business arrives with a specific question, reads pages that answer plainly and publish the price, and books a conversation without being tracked, profiled or funnelled. An AI agent evaluating Automancer receives the same truth in the forms machines read, and can quote, price and act on it correctly — because a business whose whole pitch is that agents run it should be readable by them. What a machine is told never differs from what a person is shown. The site's authority comes from a rule the whole estate shares: nothing on it is invented, inflated, or faked for a score.

## The role of AI

**Agents write it, and agents read it.** Both halves matter, and both are held to the same standard:

- **Written by the fleet, sourced from the truth layer.** Field notes and pages answer real questions a real buyer asks, and every number in them comes from evidence held elsewhere in the estate — the site publishes, it never originates a claim.
- **Readable by the fleet.** The site's own facts are published as data an agent can fetch and cite, with stable identifiers, so being quoted accurately is a property of the site rather than a matter of luck.
- **Enquiries met quickly.** What arrives is understood and a reply is drafted fast, because the promise made here is a conversation with a person, soon.
- **Its own honesty audited.** The site is continuously re-read for anything that overstates, drifts from reality, or claims a capability it does not have.

The boundary: **nothing reaches the public without Waseem.** Shipping the site is ungated; speaking to a prospect is not. And no page is ever written to pass a check it does not deserve to pass — failing honestly beats passing fraudulently, every time.

## Non-negotiables

- **No tracking.** No analytics or advertising cookies, no lead database on the site. Privacy is a feature a visitor can verify for themselves.
- **Prices are published.** What things cost is on the page, not behind a call.
- **Equal, truthful access for people and machines.** The two views can never disagree, because neither is allowed to say what the other cannot.
- **Never fake a capability for a score.** Where the host or the truth will not allow a thing, the site says so rather than simulating it.
- **Claims come from the truth layer.** The site is a publisher, not an author of facts about the business.
- **Published identifiers are promises.** What machines depend on stays stable; presentation is free to change.

## Not this

- Not a lead-capture machine dressed as a website.
- Not a content treadmill: pages ship when they are true and useful, on no one's quota.
- Not a web application. The moment it needs accounts, sessions or a database, that thing belongs in a different repository.
- Not a place to make a claim first and find the evidence later.

## Judging a change against this vision

- Could a visitor or an auditor catch the site tracking, inflating, or faking anything?
- Do people and machines still receive the same truth?
- Does every number on it still trace to evidence someone else holds?
- Does it keep the site light, fast, and honest about its limits?
