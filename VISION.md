## The destination

Automancer's public front door, trusted equally by people and by machines. A
UK small business finds it through a specific question, reads pages that
answer plainly in Automancer's own voice, and enquires without being tracked,
profiled or funnelled. An AI agent evaluating Automancer receives the same
truth, offered in the forms machines read — and what a machine is told never
differs from what a person is shown. The site's authority comes from a rule
the whole estate shares: nothing on it is invented, inflated, or faked for a
score.

## What it feels like when it works

- A prospect arrives from a long-tail search, gets a straight answer, and
  the enquiry form just works.
- An agent cites Automancer accurately without rendering a single page.
- Pages load instantly anywhere, because they carry nothing they don't need.
- An honesty audit finds nothing to catch — including honest admissions of
  what the site does not do.

## Non-negotiables

- **No tracking.** No analytics or advertising cookies, no lead database on
  the site. Privacy is a feature prospects can verify.
- **Equal, truthful access for people and machines.** The two views can
  never disagree, because neither is allowed to say what the other cannot.
- **Never fake a capability for a score.** Failing an honesty or readiness
  check truthfully beats passing it fraudulently.
- **Claims come from the BD truth layer**; the site publishes, it does not
  originate.

## Not this

- Not a lead-capture machine dressed as a website.
- Not a CMS or a blog treadmill: content ships when it is true and useful.
- Not a web application. The moment it needs accounts, sessions or a
  database, that thing belongs in a different repo.

## Judging a change against this vision

- Could a visitor or an auditor catch the site tracking, inflating, or
  faking anything?
- Do people and machines still receive the same truth?
- Does it keep the site light, fast, and honest about its limits?
